## Where does it hurt?

The location of the pain is the first clue. Pain at the front, around the kneecap, usually has different causes from pain on the inner side, the outer side or at the back. Morning stiffness across the whole knee points somewhere else again.

Use the [knee pain map](/guides/knee-pain-by-location) to see what each area usually means, and what your doctor is likely to check next.

## Wear and tear (osteoarthritis)

Osteoarthritis is wear and tear of the joint. The smooth cartilage that covers the ends of the bones slowly thins, and the knee becomes painful and stiff. The inner side of the knee is a common place for it to start. It often affects both knees.

Osteoarthritis does not always get steadily worse. Many people have good months and bad months, and the right exercise can make a real difference to how the knee feels.

## "Is it just age?"

Many people are told their knee pain is just age, and that nothing can be done. Getting older does make osteoarthritis more common. But age alone does not tell you what is causing your pain, or what will help.

A knee that hurts on stairs at 50 may have a different cause from one that is stiff every morning at 65. A careful examination can tell the difference.

## When to see a surgeon

See an orthopaedic surgeon if you have pain that has not improved in a few weeks, pain that stops you walking, climbing stairs or sleeping, a knee that locks, gives way or swells, or a knee that is slowly changing shape.

## How we decide on treatment

The plan depends on the person. I look at how much the pain limits you, what you want to get back to, what the examination and any X-rays show, and your general health. Not every case needs surgery.

Many people with knee osteoarthritis do well with physiotherapy, strengthening exercise, weight management where it helps, and pain relief advised by a doctor. When pain and stiffness keep limiting daily life despite these, we talk about other options, including [joint replacement](/what-i-treat/joint-replacement). Whatever I recommend, I will explain why.
