## ACL tear

The ACL (anterior cruciate ligament) is one of the main ligaments inside the knee. It keeps the shin bone from sliding forward and helps the knee stay stable when you twist, land or change direction. It is often injured in football and badminton, without anyone touching you.

Many people can walk after an ACL tear, so being able to walk does not rule one out. These are the signs of a possible ACL tear:

## Meniscus tear

The meniscus is a C-shaped cushion of cartilage between the thigh bone and the shin bone. Each knee has two, one on the inner side and one on the outer side. A meniscus can tear with a twist while the foot is planted, or thin with age. Common signs are pain on one side of the knee, swelling, stiffness, and a knee that catches or locks.

## Other ligament injuries

The ligaments on the inner side (MCL) and outer side (LCL) of the knee can be sprained or torn by a blow to the side of the knee or a sudden twist. Many of these heal with a period of protection and physiotherapy, but they still need a proper examination, because they can happen together with an ACL or meniscus tear.

## Why it matters

Playing on an unstable knee can slowly but surely affect other parts of the knee, like the cartilage or the meniscus, leading to irreversible damage.

## When to see a surgeon

See an orthopaedic surgeon after a sports injury where you felt a pop, if the knee gives way, locks or swells, or if the pain has not improved in a few weeks. If you are not sure it is safe to play, that is reason enough to get it examined.

## How we decide on treatment

Not every ACL tear needs surgery. The right plan depends on your knee, your sport and your goals.

I look at how the injury happened, how stable the knee is on examination, what any scans show, your age and work, and the sport you want to go back to. Some people do well with a structured physiotherapy programme. Others are advised to have the ligament rebuilt (ACL reconstruction), which is usually done by keyhole surgery. Physiotherapy-led rehabilitation is central to either path, and I work closely with physiotherapists.

## Return to sport

Return to sport after an ACL tear is an individual decision. It requires a detailed consultation, clinical examination, review of imaging, and a discussion of your sporting goals and expectations.

## Sevens season

Sevens football season runs from November to May. If you are injured during the season, stop playing and get the knee examined before your next match.
