## Four signs of a possible ACL tear

  items={[

## Why it matters

Playing on an unstable knee can slowly but surely affect other parts of the knee, like the cartilage or the meniscus, leading to irreversible damage.

## What to do

Stop playing and get the knee examined. Not every ACL tear needs surgery. The right plan depends on your knee, your sport and your goals.

## Return to sport

Return to sport after an ACL tear is an individual decision. It requires a detailed consultation, clinical examination, review of imaging, and a discussion of your sporting goals and expectations.
