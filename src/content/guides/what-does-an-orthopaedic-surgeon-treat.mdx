## What is orthopaedics?

Orthopaedics is the care of your bones, joints, ligaments, tendons and muscles. Everything that helps you move. An orthopaedic surgeon is the doctor many people call a bone doctor. You may also see it spelt "orthopedic". It is the same specialty.
