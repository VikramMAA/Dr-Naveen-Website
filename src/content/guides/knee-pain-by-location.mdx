faqs:
  - question: Is clicking in the knee normal?
    answer: Painless clicking is common and usually normal. If the knee clicks painfully, locks or gives way, get it checked.
  - question: What is patellofemoral pain?
    answer: Patellofemoral pain is pain around or behind the kneecap, where the kneecap meets the thigh bone. It is often worse on stairs, when squatting or after sitting for long.
  - question: Why does my knee hurt on stairs?
    answer: Pain on stairs often comes from the front of the knee, around or behind the kneecap (patellofemoral pain). Wear and tear can also cause it. If it has not improved in a few weeks, get it examined.
