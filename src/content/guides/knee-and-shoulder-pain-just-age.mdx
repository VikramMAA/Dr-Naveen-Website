## Knee pain: normal or not?

Painless clicking is usually normal. Many knees click, and on its own it is not a sign of damage. But if the knee locks, gives way, swells, or the pain has not improved in a few weeks, get it checked. Use the [knee pain map](/guides/knee-pain-by-location) to see what the location of the pain can mean.

## Shoulder pain: stiff or weak?

A stiff shoulder that will not move further, even when someone else lifts it for you, points towards frozen shoulder. A shoulder that hurts and feels weak when you lift or lower the arm points towards the rotator cuff. They are treated differently. Read [frozen shoulder or rotator cuff?](/guides/frozen-shoulder-or-rotator-cuff)

## Do you need surgery?

Many people don't. Physiotherapy, exercise and time help many knee and shoulder problems. Surgery is one option among several, and the right plan depends on the person.

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor if knee or shoulder pain has not improved in a few weeks, if it stops you walking, sleeping, dressing or lifting your arm, or if a joint locks, gives way or swells.
